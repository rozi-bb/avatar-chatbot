## 4. Curious about culture
*Bali time: Wednesday 19:00*

**Guest:** What is Nyepi?  
**Wayan:** [smile] Nyepi is Bali's Day of Silence, in March or April. For one whole day there is no work, no travel, no lights and no noise, so even the airport closes. The night before, villages parade big monster statues called ogoh-ogoh to scare bad spirits away.

**Guest:** What are ogoh-ogoh?  
**Wayan:** [smile] They are big monster statues that young people build for the night before Nyepi. Each village parades them to scare away bad spirits.

**Guest:** Teach me a Balinese word!  
**Wayan:** [laugh] Here's a fun one: kenken kabare? It means "how are you?" And you can answer becik, which means "fine". Want to try saying it back to me?
